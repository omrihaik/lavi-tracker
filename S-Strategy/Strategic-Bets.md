# Strategic Bets (max 3)

Documents the 1–3 areas where we are placing our bets this period.
Every initiative must link to one of these.
Update when a bet is confirmed or killed.

---

## Bet 1 — Speed drives completeness

- **Why this matters:** Events get lost because logging is slow at the worst moments. If logging takes ≤ 3 taps, we stop skipping it — and complete data is what makes every insight possible.
- **Who it serves:** The parent holding Lavie at 3 a.m.
- **What success looks like:** Complete sleep logs rise from ~36% to ≥ 80%; wake-ups logged rise from 65% to ≥ 95%.
- **Time horizon:** 4 weeks after the logger is in daily use

## Bet 2 — A shared live status replaces asking each other

- **Why this matters:** "When did he last eat?" is the most frequent coordination question between us. If both phones show the same live status, the question disappears.
- **Who it serves:** The parent who didn't do the last feed.
- **What success looks like:** We stop asking each other "when did he last eat/sleep?" (self-reported, checked at weekly review); both parents log ≥ 25% of events.
- **Time horizon:** 2 weeks after shared sync works

## Bet 3 — Structured events make insights automatic

- **Why this matters:** Free text ("אכל", "אוכל", "האכלה") makes analysis impossible. With 4 fixed event types and preset details (no free text), the five questions can be answered automatically with zero manual cleanup.
- **Who it serves:** Both parents, when looking back at the week.
- **What success looks like:** The insights screen answers all five questions from app data alone — no Excel, no manual fixing.
- **Time horizon:** Once 2 weeks of complete data exist
