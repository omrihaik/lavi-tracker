# Personas

The two users of Lavi Tracker. Names are omitted — this repo is public.

---

## Persona 1 — The Logger (Parent 1)

- **When:** Logs throughout the day — not concentrated at night.
- **Main job:** Logging, far more than looking things up.
- **Tech comfort:** High — tries every new app. Will install the PWA, explore settings, tolerate early bugs.
- **Biggest pain today:**
  1. Scrolling the Excel to find the right row.
  2. Typing details by hand — how the feeding went, ml amount, how the put-down went.
- **Implications for design:**
  - The home screen *is* the logger — no navigation before the log buttons.
  - Details (ml, crying yes/no) are taps on preset choices, never typing.
  - Time defaults to "now", editable after the fact.
