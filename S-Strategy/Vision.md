# Vision

Update at a product pivot or quarterly.

## Product in one sentence

LavieTracker lets two parents log Lavie's sleep, feeding and activity in 2–3 taps from their phones, and see at a glance what's happening now and whether things are getting better.

## Vision (2–5 lines)

Today we track Lavie in an Excel file. At 3 a.m., with a baby in one arm, finding the right row and typing "fed" is slow — so events get skipped, and the data ends up too messy to answer the questions we actually care about.

LavieTracker replaces the spreadsheet with a phone-first logger that both parents share in real time. Logging takes seconds, "when did he last eat?" is answered without asking, and the data turns into simple answers on one screen: is he sleeping enough compared to his own trend, which bedtime gets him to sleep fastest, is it getting better week to week, how feedings are going, and whether activity helps him sleep.

## What we will NOT do

- No medical data (weight, temperature, medication, doctor visits)
- No complex data entry — every log is 2–3 taps; anything that needs typing is optional
- No "more data" for its own sake — only 4 event types (sleep, feeding, put-down, activity); bath and diapers are out
- No durations or timers for activities — one tap that it happened
- No free text — every detail is a preset choice
- No dashboard per activity — one insights screen that answers the five questions
- No comparison to age norms — Lavie is compared only to himself
- No AI advice or recommendations on how to parent — the app shows patterns, we decide
