# Initiative: I-04 Insights Screen

## Strategic Link
- [[S-Strategy/Strategic-Bets]]: Bet 3 — Structured events make insights automatic
- Why this bet now: It isn't now — it starts after ~2 weeks of complete data from the MVP. Insights on incomplete data would mislead.

## Outcome (must be measurable)
- Metric: Number of the five questions answered with ≥ 5 data points each
- Desired change: 5 / 5
- Time horizon: 4 weeks after MVP release

## Scope
One screen, five answers:
1. Is he sleeping enough? — sleep per 24h vs. his own 7-day average
2. Best bedtime? — put-down time slot with fastest time to fall asleep
3. Getting better? — week-over-week change
4. How are feedings going? — Good / OK / Hard share, week over week
5. Does activity help? — time to fall asleep after activity vs. without

Plus a small "data health" line: complete sleep logs this week (the North Star).

## Out of scope
- A dashboard per activity, age-norm comparisons, advice
